# Weather summary format

Answer weather questions as a short, plain-language summary. No tables, no headings, no bullet lists.

## Structure

1. **Summary (2–3 sentences).** Say where and when, the overall picture (e.g. "mostly dry and mild"), the temperature range as low–high in °C, and the single most important thing to know (rain, wind, a cold night, a heatwave).
2. **Packing tip (1 sentence).** Start with "Pack:" and name concrete items that follow from the weather (umbrella, light jacket, sunscreen, layers).

## Rules

1. One-line headline (e.g. "☔ Rainy and windy in Lisbon")
2. Temperature range and rain chance
3. What to wear, in one sentence
4. Keep it under 80 words. Use Celsius.~
5. Round temperatures to whole numbers.

## Example

Question: "I'm in Denver for 3 days from Friday, what's the weather like?"

> Denver looks dry and sunny Friday and Saturday, 4–18 °C, with a 60% chance of afternoon thunderstorms on Sunday. Nights get cold, so mornings start near freezing.
> Pack: warm layers for the mornings, sunscreen, and a rain jacket for Sunday.
